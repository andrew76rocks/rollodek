## Class Ability: Light the Way

**Rules text:**
Once per scene, after a check is failed (yours or an ally's), you may flip the top card of your hero deck. If it's a **Dexterity** card, the check succeeds instead.

**Flavor:** She's lit a hundred lanterns. She only has to be right once.

**Design note:** A "save" moment that feels earned, not guaranteed. Roughly 40 to 50% hit rate with her starting deck, so it's exciting but not a sure thing.
