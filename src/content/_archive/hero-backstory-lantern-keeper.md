# Kessa Vantree: Backstory Card

## Card Face

**Title:** The Lantern Keeper's Daughter

**Flavor text (short, for the card):**
> She grew up lighting the way for others. Now she walks the dark alone.

## Backstory

Kessa was raised at the edge of the Hollow Wood, where her father kept the lanterns that guided travelers along the old road. Every night she walked the line with him, learning which paths were safe and which ones only looked that way.

When she was sixteen, a caravan went into the Wood on a night her father was too sick to walk. Kessa lit the lanterns alone. She lit one wrong. The caravan never came out.

Her father took the blame and the town let him. He died a few winters later, still owing the caravan families a debt he swore to repay. Kessa left with his lantern, his map, and his promise. She hasn't been home since.

She's quiet, careful, and good in the wild. She doesn't trust roads she hasn't walked herself, and she doesn't trust people who make promises easily.

## Strength: Trail Sense

**Rules text:**
When you play a **Dexterity** card on a check to track, scout, find a path, or move through the wild, add **+1** to the Card Value.

**Flavor:** She reads the ground like other people read a page.

**Design note:** Small, frequent bonus. Fires often in exploration scenes, rarely in social ones. Pushes players to lean into her Dexterity cards.

## Weakness: The Unpaid Oath

**Rules text:**
When the Adventure Deck reveals a card tagged **Debt**, **Caravan**, or **Home**, Kessa must choose one:
- Take on the side quest (add it to your tableau), or
- Take **1 Wound** card.

**Flavor:** Every lantern she passes reminds her what she still owes.

**Design note:** Not a stat penalty. It's a story hook that forces a choice and creates tension instead of just making her worse at things.

## Story Hooks (for Adventure Deck authors)

- A survivor from the lost caravan turns up alive.
- Someone is lighting the Hollow Wood lanterns again.
- A creditor comes to collect her father's debt.
- Her father's map has a path on it she's never seen.
